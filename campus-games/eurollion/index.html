Popular: higher scores win. 100+ scores Excellent; 90–99 Very nice; 70–89 Good; 50–69 Not bad; 0–49 Keep trying.

Daily gives you seven fixed questions for your topic, refreshed at midnight Paris time (Europe/Paris). Each has 25 seconds. Practice offers fresh selections and an optional timer. Changing from Rare to Popular does not create new daily questions.
