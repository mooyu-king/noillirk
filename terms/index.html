Scores and availability

Scores are based on limited original test mappings. They have no monetary value, do not represent survey percentages, and do not establish original-game rankings. The standard local API retains unexpired state across restarts. Storage faults, expiry or removal of the local database can still affect access. Do not rely on this development build for permanent records.

Fair and respectful use

Use appropriate nicknames. Do not harass others, impersonate an operator, interfere with rooms or attempt to access someone else's credentials. Do not submit harmful content or private information.

Content and external games

Names and marks remain with their respective owners. Links to external games do not imply a partnership, license or control over those services. No permission to reuse a third party's answer library is granted by this site.

Feedback

You may send corrections for review. A report does not promise acceptance, a response time or retrospective score changes. Only submit material you have permission to share, with unnecessary personal information removed.
