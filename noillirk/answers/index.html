What will be accepted?

The exact English answer or a listed equivalent must match the current question’s mapping. Case and extra spaces do not change acceptance. A spelling that is reasonable elsewhere is not automatically an alias here. Chinese support means explicitly listed equivalents, not general Chinese-language judging. Pinyin and automatic translation are not connected.

Find your next step.

Check the exact question, use an English answer or a listed Chinese equivalent, and try a displayed example. Not every reasonable answer is covered. AI judging and automatic translations are not connected.

Use the preview without guessing the database

  1. Choose Crowd preview and read the category and exact prompt.
  2. Use a visible example chip if you want to test the animation and score reliably.
  3. Submit with Lock in or end the wait with Skip wait.
  4. Read the revealed count and score, then continue to the next question.
  5. For the full review, use the standalone game; the compact homepage frame keeps the main controls in view.

Visible examples make this a demonstration, not a competitive leaderboard. Selecting an example is not evidence of public popularity. See one complete scoring example.

How to report a coverage gap

Send the exact question, your answer, the question version and what the screen showed to our contact address. Remove nicknames, credentials and private information. Email is the available feedback channel; no upload form, automatic approval or AI review is active.

What a trustworthy answer record needs

A future record needs a question definition, a canonical answer, reviewed aliases, source and permitted use, collection date, population and sample method. Counts must belong to the same eligible response set. Weighted percentages cannot be silently converted into exact people. Editorial estimates, observed game scores and measured responses must remain distinguishable.

Corrections should create reviewed versions rather than rewriting an active run. Search can help find candidate answers and references, but neither a result’s position nor the number of matching web pages measures how many people would answer it.
