Eraly-style history game: guess the year

Looking for an Eraly-style daily history challenge? Play The Year Archive, our independent year-guessing game, with seven historical clues, a daily round and untimed practice.

The Year Archive

What is the Eraly year-guessing game?

Eraly is a daily history guessing game built around a simple question: what year did this happen? You read an event, enter a year and compare your guess with the actual date. Players searching for Eraly often want a quick history puzzle, an explanation of the rules or another round of year-guessing practice.

Here on Noillirk Games, The Year Archive offers an independently developed version of that challenge. Choose Daily for seven clues with 25 seconds each, or Practice to think without a timer. The game above uses its own questions, character and scoring rules.

How to play this history game

Fern keeps a shelf of undated records. Each card describes a real event. Enter the year you think it happened, then open the record to see how far your guess landed from the mark. Read Fern’s note before moving on. The next clock starts only when you turn the page.

How does scoring work in this Eraly-style game?

Your score is the absolute difference in years. Guess 1900 for an event in 2000 and score 100. Lower is better; an exact answer scores zero. Timeout adds 1000 points. Fern reacts to 0 (Excellent), 1–10 (Very nice), 11–30 (Good), 31–50 (Average), and over 50 (Poor). Your final reaction uses the average across seven records; the ledger shows the total and every answer.

Can I play more after the daily challenge?

Daily gives everyone the same seven records for the UTC date and allows 25 seconds per question. Practice draws another set and leaves the clock off. You can start another practice round after finishing. Questions come from a finite bank, so familiar clues can return. All answers use positive, common-era years.

Can I finish later?

On the same browser, choose Daily again to reopen today’s ledger if local storage is available. A running clock keeps counting while you are away. Leave after revealing an answer to pause between records. Practice starts fresh each time.

Tips for guessing historical years

Start by placing the clue in a century, then narrow it to a decade. Link inventions, expeditions and discoveries to dates you already remember. Read the exact event: a first flight, a first passenger service and a first landing can belong to different years. In Daily, enter your best estimate before the clock ends; in Practice, take time to reason through the clue.

After each reveal, compare your estimate with the actual year and read the short explanation. This turns a missed date into a useful reference for the next round.

Eraly-style game FAQ

Is this the official Eraly game?

This page hosts The Year Archive, an independent history game by Noillirk Games. Its lower-is-better scoring, question bank and character belong to this edition.

Can I play for free without an account?

Yes. Start the game above in your browser. Daily and Practice are available without signing in.

Where can I see today's answers?

Each completed question reveals its actual year and an explanation. The final ledger lists your seven guesses and their answers so you can review your round.

What is a perfect score?

Zero total error is perfect in The Year Archive. Every exact year adds zero; each year of difference adds one point. A timed-out question adds 1000 points.
