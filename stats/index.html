What counts would need before public use

  • A permitted, identifiable source and exact question wording.
  • Collection dates, language, geography, sample definition and single- or multiple-answer method.
  • Distinct labels for raw counts, weighted shares and editorial estimates.
  • Reviewed alias merging, preserved raw provenance and a versioned correction history.
  • Exclusion rules for development runs, duplicates and abuse, with known sampling limits disclosed.

Game submissions can be strategic: someone trying to maximize points is not necessarily reporting their own preference. Future player aggregates must not be marketed as a representative survey. No leaderboard or “players online” number is invented here.

Can search results provide the database?

Search is useful for discovering evidence and candidate answers. A high search ranking is not a respondent, and a search-result count is not a vote total. A source may support that an answer is relevant without providing the frequencies needed for proportional scoring. Those two kinds of evidence must not be collapsed into one confidence number.
