A BETTER NEXT ANSWER

Noillirk tips: familiar, specific, on time.

Match the category first.

If a prompt asks for a fruit, an everyday fruit fits; a drink made from fruit may not. Read the whole prompt before chasing a popular answer.

Pick one clear idea.

Use one answer, not a list such as “apple or banana.” Extra alternatives do not give you extra guesses and may have no mapping.

Submit before the deadline.

Typing is not submitting. Press Enter or Submit and check the response. There is no speed bonus, but waiting until the final instant risks arriving too late.

These are practical habits, not guaranteed top answers. This edition uses fixed test mappings, not live votes from your room.

What should I do when…?

My answer is reasonable, but it says UNMAPPED.

The current question version has no accepted mapping for that input. Check the category and try one different answer while time remains. Repeating the same input cannot improve its score. Only listed aliases count; the system does not judge new answers with AI.

After the run, use the review’s mapping feedback link. A report does not immediately change the board or your score.

My answer was accepted, but earned fewer points than expected.

Accepted means the answer exists in the table, not that it is the top answer. Open the revealed board and compare its tier with your result. The score is fixed by that question version, not your speed or the number of players who chose it.

You cannot replace a locked answer. Use untimed practice to learn the flow without a deadline.

I typed an answer, but the round ended with zero.

An unsubmitted answer does not count. A blank or unmapped timeout adds no points. Skip wait ends waiting and preserves the current answer: listed answers score normally, while unlisted answers are shown as not scored. If your last submitted attempt was unmapped, its text remains in the review. A refresh does not pause the clock.

The game service is unavailable.

Full games currently require the local API; this is not a hosted public game service. A connection failure is not a wrong answer. Do not repeatedly submit or assume refreshing resets your timer.

You can still use the score calculator and read the rules without the game API. To play the original hosted edition, follow the PlayDaily link on the homepage.

LESS GUESSWORK ABOUT THE RULES

Noillirk questions, answered

How long is a game, and what is the highest score?

A standard run has seven questions with up to 15 seconds each: up to 105 seconds of answer time, plus countdowns and reveals. A round may reveal early once everyone has locked an answer. Each question is worth up to 100 points, so the maximum is 700. Practice has no timer. Private rooms also offer a separate 14-round format.

Can I change an answer or try again?

An accepted answer locks for that round. If your answer is not in this version’s mapping, it is not accepted and you can try again before the server deadline. UNMAPPED means missing coverage, not proof that your answer is wrong.

What happens if I skip or run out of time?

Skip wait ends the remaining wait and keeps your answer. Listed answers score normally; unlisted answers are saved as not scored. It does not throw away a scored answer. An unanswered timeout adds no points.

Do plurals, typos and synonyms count?

Capitalization, spacing and Unicode are normalized. The updated test bank includes reviewed English variants and Chinese equivalents. Only explicitly listed aliases are accepted; not every plural, typo or synonym is supported, and pinyin is not inferred. If a reasonable answer is missing, use the mapping feedback link in your final review.

When does Daily reset? Is it the PlayDaily puzzle?

Our independent test set changes at midnight UTC. The game displays the next reset in your local time. It does not retrieve PlayDaily’s daily puzzle. Replays are practice, not entries in an official daily leaderboard.

Will a refresh save my progress?

The current tab keeps a session credential when browser storage is available, and the local API retains unexpired games. Refreshing does not pause the timer. Recovery is not guaranteed after closing the tab, clearing storage, expiration or losing the local database; there is no cross-device account sync.

Can I play with friends?

Private test rooms support 2–12 players using the same local API. Late joiners spectate until the next match; tied scores share a place. Public multiplayer hosting, accounts and an authenticated review dashboard are not active.

Are these official answers or real survey results?

No. The game currently uses 21 independent development questions with limited answer mappings. The score lab is a separate fictional lesson. Neither is a PlayDaily answer database, and 100 points does not mean 100% of people.
